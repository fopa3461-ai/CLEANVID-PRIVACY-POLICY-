Utility Box Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Utility Box respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Utility Box application.

1. Information We Collect

Utility Box does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect or store your personal information on our servers.

Images, text, and QR codes processed using Utility Box are designed to be processed locally on your device.

2. Your Files and Content

Utility Box does not upload your images, text, or QR code content to our servers.

Your content remains on your device unless you choose to save, export, or share it using your device or another application.

3. Advertising

Utility Box may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use certain information from your device for purposes such as displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and maintaining the security of their services.

Advertising information may include device-related information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, depending on your device settings and applicable laws.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's privacy policies and advertising information.

4. Local Processing

The primary image, text, and QR code tools are designed to process user content locally on the device. User content is not intentionally transmitted to Utility Box servers as part of normal operation.

5. Children's Privacy

Utility Box does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Because user content is processed locally, Utility Box does not maintain a server-side database containing users' images, text, or QR code content.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Utility Box's features, advertising services, or data practices change. The latest version will be made available on this webpage.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Utility Box, please contact us through the contact information provided with the application.
